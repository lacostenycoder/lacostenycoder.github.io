Historical Pre-1970 Embryology Textbooks

Selected passages from medical and embryology texts published before 1970 describing fertilization and the formation or beginning of a new individual.

1

Human Embryology

Bradley M. Patten · 3rd Edition · 1968 · p. 43 · McGraw-Hill

“It is the penetration of the ovum by a spermatozoon and the resultant mingling of the nuclear material each brings to the union that constitutes the culmination of the process of fertilization and marks the initiation of the life of a new individual.”

View source at Internet Archive

2

Developmental Anatomy: A Textbook and Laboratory Manual of Human Embryology

Leslie Brainerd Arey · 7th Edition · 1965 · p. 55 · W.B. Saunders

“The formation, maturation and meeting of a male and female sex cell are all preliminary to their actual union into a combined cell, or zygote, which definitely marks the beginning of a new individual.”

View source at Internet Archive

3

Pathology of the Fetus and the Infant

Edith L. Potter · 1st Edition · 1952 · p. 14 · Year Book Publishers

“Every time a sperm cell unites with an ovum a new life is initiated and a new individual comes into existence.”

View source at HathiTrust

4

Ourselves Unborn: An Introduction to Human Embryology

George W. Corner · 1944 · p. 2 · Yale University Press

“At the moment of fertilization, when the sperm fuses with the egg, a single cell is formed. From this single cell, the new individual develops.”
