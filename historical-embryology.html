1968

Human Embryology

Bradley M. Patten · 3rd Edition · p. 43 · McGraw-Hill

“It is the penetration of the ovum by a spermatozoon and the resultant mingling of the nuclear material each brings to the union that constitutes the culmination of the process of fertilization and marks the initiation of the life of a new individual.”

How to use these quotations responsibly

Historical textbook wording can be useful for understanding how embryologists described fertilization and individual development in different eras. A quotation should be checked against its edition and surrounding text before being used in research or debate. Where a source is available online, this site links directly to it.

Historical source material presented for documentary and educational reference. Scientific terminology and historical wording should be read in context; biological terminology does not by itself resolve legal, moral, religious, or philosophical questions.
